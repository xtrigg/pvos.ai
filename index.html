The gap

Voice AI is powerful. Raw voice data is dangerous.

Most AI recorders upload audio first and solve privacy later. PVOS.ai moves the privacy decision to the customer edge: local ASR, PII detection, policy scoring, redaction, and routing happen before any third-party model sees the content.

Architecture

Three layers, one turnkey edge deployment.

01

AI Recorder

Wearable, desk, vehicle, or room microphones capture field conversations, meetings, service calls, and sales interactions.

  • Always-available capture
  • Device fleet management
  • Local buffering and secure sync
02

Privacy Gateway

The customer-side gateway runs transcription, PII detection, policy rules, and Privacy Score routing before data leaves the network.

  • Mask PII and secrets
  • Keep P3 content local only
  • Audit every redaction decision
03

Memory OS

Safe context becomes searchable knowledge, summaries, CRM notes, task triggers, and AI agent memory across the company.

  • Team memory workspace
  • Customer-owned model routing
  • API and workflow integrations
